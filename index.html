MERGE SORT

New here? Take 20 seconds.

Merge sort sorts a list by splitting it into tiny pieces (a single number is always "sorted"), then merging the pieces back together - each merge picks the smallest front value first, so the result comes out in order.

✂️SPLIT
Keep cutting the list in half until every piece holds one number.
🧪MERGE
Pair up the pieces, always taking the smaller front value - like combining two sorted piles of cards.
✅SORTED
When every piece is merged back, the whole list is in order. Guaranteed.

You control the pace: Play runs it, Next / Back move one move at a time, and a friendly card explains every single move in plain words.
